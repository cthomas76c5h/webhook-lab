Zapier app flow

With our own Zapier app, the firm never copies a URL. When they turn their Zap on, Zapier calls POST /v1/hooks with its own hook URL. When they turn it off or delete it, Zapier calls DELETE /v1/hooks/:id. The Test step calls GET /v1/samples/lead.created, so sample data is always complete and nobody has to send a lead at the right moment.

These buttons make the same calls Zapier would, with the firm's API key.

    What this lab explores
    
    
      
      
    • At least once, with dedupe. Every retry carries the same webhook-id.
      • 
      
    • Signed requests. Standard Webhooks headers; rotate a secret and watch both signatures go out.
      • 
      
    • Clear failure rules. 2xx done; 408/425/429/5xx/no answer retry with backoff and Retry-After; 410 switches the endpoint off; other 4xx and 3xx stop at once.
      • 
      
    • Free-form bodies. Templates, checked against a full and a sparse lead.
      • 
      
    • No URL hand-off. The Zapier app flow subscribes and unsubscribes by itself.
      • 
      
    • Safe addresses. https only and no private networks (localhost is allowed for the fake receivers).
      • 
    
    
    
    Time runs fast: retries that take hours in real life take seconds here.
